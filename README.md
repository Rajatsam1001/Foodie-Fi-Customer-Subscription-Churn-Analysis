
# 🍽️ Foodie-Fi Customer Subscription & Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
Foodie-Fi is a subscription-based video streaming platform offering exclusive food content through multiple subscription plans.

The objective of this project is to analyze customer subscription behavior using SQL and answer key business questions related to customer acquisition, subscription upgrades, churn, annual plan conversion, and customer journeys.

This project demonstrates analytical thinking, business understanding, and advanced SQL querying techniques relevant to Data Analyst roles.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze customer acquisition and subscription trends.
- Calculate customer churn rates and identify churn patterns.
- Evaluate monthly trial distribution and post-trial customer journeys.
- Analyze subscription plan upgrades and annual plan conversions.
- Measure the average time taken to upgrade to an annual plan.
- Identify subscription downgrades and customer retention opportunities.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### Customer Retention & Churn Analysis
- Total Customer Analysis
- Churn Rate Calculation
- Churn Immediately After Trial

### Subscription & Conversion Analysis

- Annual Plan Upgrade Analysis

This analysis helps identify customer churn patterns, understand subscription conversion behavior, evaluate plan preferences, and uncover opportunities to improve customer retention and annual subscription adoption.
